BROWN & CO

Every light of the season.

A holiday campaign pitched to Brown & Co. Jewelers in Buckhead and Roswell. Every piece is built on light: billboards that outshine the season, a print promise, an in-store proof, and a finale that puts the customer's own proposal in lights.

01

The insight

The insight

Jewelry is chosen under showroom lights. It's worn under every other kind.

The idea

Every light of the season.

02

Out-of-home: it outshines the season

Every holiday light needs a plug, a battery or a box in the attic. The diamond doesn't.

03

Print: it makes the promise

The spread turns the idea into a claim: Brown & Co. tests every stone in the light it will actually live in, not just the light of the showroom.

04

In-store: it proves it

The Lighting Bar brings the claim to the counter: five lighting booths, from string lights to the phone flash, so customers see a stone in every light before they buy. The sixth booth and a card on the counter sell the finale: Your Name in Lights.

05

The final light: your name in lights

First, the space goes dark. One board across Buckhead runs almost empty, holding a place for a name. Then customers fill it: buy an engagement ring in December, and Brown & Co. lights that board with one question. The campaign starts on billboards and ends on one, only now the billboard belongs to the customer, and every proposal becomes a story the couple shares for us.

  1. Choose the ring. Engagement rings purchased December 1–24 qualify.
  2. Choose the night. The customer books a time slot and a first name, approved by Brown & Co.
  3. Light it up. The question runs on a digital board over Buckhead, and Brown & Co. captures the moment for the couple to share.

Every light of the season.

The brightest one has her name on it.
