The world

In 2187, synthetic won. Diamonds are infinite, identical and Bureau approved. Even a proposal is something you grab on the way to your gate.

04

The system

A world this perfect needs enforcing. The Synthetic Bureau controls what people see, what counts as real and, finally, who they love. Each of its tools is a live piece of the campaign.

The Broadcasts

It controls the story.

A Bureau news network and public announcements, one message repeated until it sticks: synthetic is better.

Watch the broadcasts: synthcity.tv →

The Rarity Detector

It erases the real.

A working AI tool. Upload any diamond and the Bureau classifies it. Natural stones are flagged as origin anomalies: non-compliant, removed from the record.

And people really use it. Real scans from real visitors: in 2187, almost everything is obsolete.

Try it: raritydetector.com →

The Synth-Mate Lab

It replaces love.

The system's final step. Visitors ride up to the Synthesis Division, upload a face scan and watch the lab grow a partner engineered for perfect compatibility: flawless, loyal and programmed never to leave. If love can be manufactured, what is it worth?

Enter the lab: syntheticbureau.com →

Meet the Clonesworths

Inside the world, the Bureau sells it like a 1950s dream. Real love is messy. Synth-Mates come home perfect: always smiling, always on time, always in the right light.
