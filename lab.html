01 · Experiential technology

PROJECT ORION

The room is the screen.

Orion projects digital imagery straight onto the objects and surfaces around you. No headset, no phone, nothing to download. A table, a wall or a shop window becomes part of the story, so retail floors, live events and brand activations become places people step into, not screens they look at.
