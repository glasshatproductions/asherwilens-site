FAGE

Honestly crafted. Nothing to hide.

A spec campaign built on one simple truth: FAGE has almost nothing in it. Milk and live active cultures. The platform takes that honesty literally, from a nearly empty billboard to a live camera inside the factory. Visuals AI-assisted.

01

The insight

The insight

FAGE's ingredient list is two lines long. So we made it the ad.

The idea

Nothing to hide.

02

Out-of-home: the label is the ad

Where other brands fill every inch, FAGE leaves the space empty. The fine print is the whole message.

03

The people behind it

If the yogurt has nothing to hide, neither do the people who make it. Real roles, real standards, named on the poster.

04

Corp-Cam

Live cameras inside the factory, the lab and the break room at fagelivecam.com. Nine feeds, around the clock. Subway posters show what the cameras catch.

05

Experiential: the confession booth

Then the brand turns the honesty back on the public. Step into the booth, confess a yogurt secret, walk out with free FAGE.

06

Packaging and social

The confessions keep going at home. Every lid lists one more ingredient, and #NothingToHide collects the rest.

07

The neighbors

Everyone in the building has a yogurt secret. Building-side billboards out the habits of the people who live there, by apartment number.
