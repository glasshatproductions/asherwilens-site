UBER

When it's time to go.

Nights don't end on schedule. This campaign meets people at the exact moment a night turns, and uses humor and self-recognition to make leaving the obvious move. Visuals AI-assisted.

01

Experiential

Escape the Bar: a pop-up where leaving the bar becomes the game.

02

On-premise / Bar activations

The Things You Say Before It's Time to Go. A collectible coaster system placed at the point of consumption, using humor and self-recognition to trigger the decision to leave before the night decides for you.

03

Out-of-home

The City Talks Back. Minimal, high-contrast placements embedded in the urban night, designed to catch attention when awareness is lowest and Uber is needed most.

04

Ambient / Object media

When the environment becomes the message. Physical interventions that turn everyday objects into moments of redirection.
